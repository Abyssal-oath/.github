## ⛧ The Oath

We are **Abyssal Oath**, a collective of developers, builders, and curious minds united by a shared obsession with creating things that matter.

We experiment. We build. We break things. We learn. Then we commit the fix.

From small utilities to ambitious software projects, we believe the best way to learn technology is to build with it.

No unnecessary hierarchy. No gatekeeping. Just people who create.

> *The abyss is vast. Our repositories are deeper.*

---

## ⚔️ Our Principles

- **Build relentlessly.** Ideas mean nothing until they become reality.
- **Learn by creating.** Every project is an opportunity to become better.
- **Share knowledge.** Good developers grow faster together.
- **Respect the craft.** Write code worth maintaining.
- **Stay curious.** Explore the unknown and question the obvious.
- **Create with purpose.** Build useful things, not just impressive demos.

## 🩸 Join the Collective

You don't need to be an expert. You need curiosity, initiative, and a willingness to learn.

We welcome developers, designers, students, and builders who want to collaborate on meaningful projects.

**You belong here if you:**

- Enjoy building things instead of only talking about them.
- Want to learn from other developers.
- Have ideas worth exploring.
- Respect other contributors and their work.
- Are willing to contribute, experiment, and improve.

### How to get involved

1. Explore our repositories.
2. Find a project that interests you.
3. Read its contribution guidelines and open issues.
4. Submit a pull request or propose an idea.
5. Help make the collective better.

Whether you're writing your first script or architecting complex systems, there's room to grow here.

---

## 🔱 The Rules of the Oath

1. Respect every contributor.
2. Keep discussions constructive.
3. Give credit where it is due.
4. Write maintainable code and meaningful documentation.
5. Never compromise security or expose private information.
6. Follow each project's license and contribution guidelines.
7. Build together. Learn together. Improve together.

The aesthetic may be dark. The community standards aren't.
